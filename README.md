## 🤔 The Problem: You Have a Shopfront, but Nobody Finds It

For a bakery in Kensington, a plumber in Dubai or a barber in Berlin, the real problem is this: **the shop exists, the customers exist, but the digital shopfront doesn't.** Your Instagram page doesn't surface when someone searches "bakery Kensington". Your WhatsApp number is a photo of a printed sheet passed around. Your price list is, at best, a blurry PDF.

So a customer searches, checks three or four Instagram accounts — one is closed, one is out of date, one has no phone number — and never walks through your door.

This platform exists to close that gap. You **type your business details once**; AI and ready-made templates handle the rest. What you get back: a storefront that opens in one click, gets indexed by Google, takes orders over WhatsApp, and ships with a QR code you print on your table.

> **NexusPages** — where 28,777 businesses said "find me".

## ❓ FAQ

**What is NexusPages?**
NexusPages is a global business directory and digital storefront platform for small and medium-sized businesses. Businesses can list for free, then upgrade to AI-generated websites, unlimited products, marketplace storefronts and custom domains.

**Is the free plan really free?**
Yes. Free is $0/year, requires no card and activates instantly. It includes a business listing, a standard profile, 5 active products and 0% commission.

**Do I build the AI website myself?**
No. You fill in your business details and the AI engine generates a complete HTML micro-site including title, description, technical specifications and 5 FAQ entries. This is available on Lite and Pro.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. Pro includes a free `.com` or `.net` domain, and you can also connect an existing domain via DNS with `ns1.carkbilisim.com` / `ns2.carkbilisim.com`. On your custom domain the storefront, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt` and `manifest.json` work automatically.

**Do you take a commission?**
No — commission is 0% on every plan. With the manual payment method, funds go directly to the business owner's account.

**Will I rank on Google?**
Claimed businesses get automatic Schema.org `LocalBusiness` data, long-tail SEO pages, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, IndexNow pings and Google Indexing API integration.

**Are QR codes and NFC cards included?**
Yes — QR codes and NFC smart business cards are included on all plans.

**How do I pay?**
Securely through Stripe Checkout / Payment Links. Alternatively, the business owner defines their own IBAN over WhatsApp and payment goes directly to the owner.

**What are Fox points for?**
You can convert Fox points into AI credits, Showcase Plus visibility and discount coupons. They never expire.

**Is my data safe?**
Yes — privacy is handled under our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service with a GDPR-oriented framework. You can request account and data deletion at any time.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Yes. The site is a full PWA and is also available on Google Play as a TWA app. Add it to your home screen and enable push notifications.
